# Implementation decisions

This file records choices needed to make the static app deterministic and safe to use.

- **Static hosting and files:** use native browser modules and fetch the eight CSV files from `data/`. GitHub Pages is the target; local use should be through an HTTP server rather than `file://`.
- **Calendar arithmetic:** validate strict `YYYY-MM-DD` dates and convert them to UTC-based whole-day ordinals. Hotel nights include check-in and exclude check-out; golf rounds can be on either the check-in or check-out date.
- **Money and rounding:** represent prices and totals as safe integer minor units. Convert each complete supplier line using the FX decimal as a rational fraction and round half up to one GBP penny. Totals sum already-rounded GBP lines; formatting uses decimal currency display only.
- **Room occupancy:** the form offers one or two occupants, bounded by the CSV `max_occupancy`. Single-occupancy price columns are mandatory. Travellers must fit the sum of room-line occupancies; spare bed capacity above 150% of travellers is treated as “considerably more” and generates a warning.
- **Automatic extras:** buggy quantity is the ceiling of players divided by two for each round. Player-round and per-room-night quantities sum over all selected rounds and room-nights. Blank override means automatic, zero is an explicit exclusion, and a positive value replaces the calculated quantity.
- **Transfers:** passenger counts are assigned exactly across one or more vehicles, each within its inclusive min/max range. The chosen combination minimizes client sell price; a supplier-cost total breaks ties. Return journeys double both vehicle prices.
- **Offers:** validity dates are inclusive. Stay-pay selects the cheapest sell-priced nights for each room line, and reduces the corresponding supplier-cost nights when cost is included. Early-booking discounts are based on the rounded GBP accommodation subtotal. Eligible offers stack in CSV order but cannot discount more than the remaining accommodation amount. Free-place uses `floor(players / (paying players + 1))` for each eligible round. Cost-only discounts remain internal and are not printed for the client.
- **Payment deadline:** a balance date on or before seven days after the quote date makes the full quote due immediately. Otherwise the deposit is due now and the remainder is due on the configured balance date.
- **Invoice numbers:** sequences are stored in browser local storage separately for each issue year and are reserved before PDF generation. Existing invoice numbers on saved or imported quotes are also considered, so a failed download leaves a skipped number rather than risking reuse. Clearing browser data or moving to another browser also clears that browser's sequence; this static app cannot coordinate numbers between devices.
- **Storage and backups:** browser local storage is the only quote store. JSON backup import merges by quote ID, replacing matching records and preserving unrelated records. Deletion requires browser confirmation.
- **Dashboard analytics:** conversion is Confirmed divided by Confirmed plus Lost. Confirmed revenue and profit exclude Draft, Sent, and Lost quotes. Average margin is the arithmetic average of saved quote margins. Grouped revenue and margin use confirmed records.
- **Data errors:** a malformed file is reported without aborting the other file fetches. Quotes can still be priced from valid portions of the data, but calculations block when the selected booking lacks a valid required price or rate.
- **Currencies and precision:** all supported currencies use two minor-unit decimal places, matching the integer-pence accounting model and current sample rates.
- **Invoice wording:** company, bank, and terms are explicit placeholders in sample settings. Replace them before sharing an invoice with a client.
